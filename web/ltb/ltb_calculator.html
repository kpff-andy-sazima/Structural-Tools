Section properties come from the AISC Shapes Database v16.0 via steelpy (generated into shapes.js). rts (Eq. F2-7), c (Eq. F2-8), rt (Eq. F4-11) and the tee Sxc/Sxt split are derived in-browser. Moments are reported in kip-ft and kip-in; lengths follow the selected unit.

The nominal strength reported is the lowest value among all limit states applicable to the section and axis, per the User Note table in Section F1. Limit states that do not apply are shown greyed out with the reason. Review the assumptions listed in the amber box before using results for design.
